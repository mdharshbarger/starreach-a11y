Mission Timeline

  1. 2025

    Concept Finalization

    Thor, Odin, and Hermes missions reach Phase B, finalizing mission architecture.

  2. 2026

    Subsystem Testing

    Prototype engines and orbital comms hardware undergo testing for Hermes and Thor.

  3. 2027

    Assembly Begins

    Hermes satellites go into production. First-stage tanks fabricated for Thor mission.

  4. 2028

    System Integration

    Final stack integration for Thor. Odin's platform heads to L2 orbit.

  5. Q2 2029

    Thor Mission Launch

    StarReach delivers 13-ton container to the Moon, beginning permanent base construction.
